  How it works
  
Four steps between an agent's idea and your cloud.

  

    
Register the agent
Every agent gets an identity: owner, model, scoped permissions, expiration. Unknown agents are denied before policy is even consulted.

    
Every action hits the gate
The agent proposes; Signplane resolves identity, intent, and blast radius. Policy decides: allow, require approval, or block.

    
Humans where it matters
Low-risk flows in milliseconds. Risky changes wait with risk score, cost delta, and blast radius. Forbidden ones never reach the cloud API.

    
Everything becomes evidence
Each action is a hash-chained Evidence Package: intent, policy, approver, diff, result. Tamper with one record and the chain says so.

Risk classification with change windows — the same change can be MEDIUM inside your Saturday maintenance window and HIGH at Tuesday noon. Approvals carry a TTL: a 2pm sign-off can't silently execute next week.

"Show me every change an AI made to production in Q3, who approved each one, and prove the log wasn't edited." Today: two weeks of grepping. With Signplane: one click, mapped to SOC 2 CC8.1 / SOX change controls.

    
03 · RECOVER

    The drift guard
    
An approval is a decision about the world at approval time.

    
Approve a change at 2pm for the 2am window, and by 2am the world may have moved — someone scaled the cluster, an incident rerouted traffic. Most schedulers fire whatever was queued. Signplane re-validates at release: approval still valid, policy window still open, cloud state still the one the approver looked at. Any drift, and the change refuses to run — back to a human, with the drift on the record.

  FAQ
  
The questions everyone asks.

  

    
Do I have to change my agents?
No. Observe-only mode is a proxy in front of what you already run. Enforcement is a flag you flip per environment, when you're ready.

    
What if Signplane is down?
Fail-closed for enforcement environments, fail-open for observe-only — you choose per environment.

    
We run change management through ServiceNow.
Keep it. Signplane's evidence packages are your change records; ticket sync is on the roadmap. What ServiceNow can't do is re-validate cloud state at release time — we do.

    
Which clouds and tools?
AWS + Azure, Terraform/OpenTofu + Kubernetes, GitHub, Slack (beta). Vendor-neutral by design — we govern the layer above your tools, not inside them.

    
Is the evidence ledger a blockchain?
No. It's an append-only hash chain — the boring, auditable kind. Each record carries the hash of the one before it; edit anything and the chain visibly breaks.
